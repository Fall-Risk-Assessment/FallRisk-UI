# NNNN — Imperative decision title

- **Recorded:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decision date:** YYYY-MM-DD, or explicitly Unknown for a retrospective record
- **Scope:** Name the affected subsystem and prototype/production boundary.
- **Decision evidence / authority:** Identify explicit approval and its source, or state that approval/deciders are not recorded.

## Status

Proposed

Use Proposed, Accepted, Rejected, Deprecated, or Superseded. Keep implementation and verification status separate. Code presence does not establish approval. For a superseded decision, link its replacement; the new record links the record it supersedes.

## Context

Explain one significant architectural problem, the relevant business/user need, constraints, and the evidence available. Separate observations from assumptions and retrospective reasoning. Reference BP/FR/NFR identifiers where applicable, explaining the relevant requirement in prose so this record remains understandable without local-only files.

## Decision

State the chosen or proposed approach and its scope. State important boundaries that future implementers must preserve. Do not turn open questions into accepted production contracts.

## Consequences

- Benefits and useful capabilities.
- Costs, limitations, risks, and follow-up work.
- A concrete condition that would trigger reconsideration.

## Alternatives

Compare relevant alternatives and why this approach was selected or is proposed. Identify retrospective analysis explicitly; do not invent prior meetings, benchmarks, or team deliberation.

## Implementation and verification

### YYYY-MM-DD — Initial record or dated implementation update

Record what exists, what was checked, results, remaining work, and unverified claims. Identify reported historical results as historical rather than claiming they were rerun. Preserve earlier accepted rationale; add dated evidence, or supersede the record when the decision changes.

## Sources

Link relevant source files, tests, requirements, related ADRs, and authoritative external references. Identify local-only sources. Exclude credentials and participant data.

Template adapted from the [Michael Nygard template](https://github.com/architecture-decision-record/architecture-decision-record/blob/main/locales/en-001/templates/decision-record-template-by-michael-nygard/index.md). This project adds metadata, alternatives, verification, and evidence references.
